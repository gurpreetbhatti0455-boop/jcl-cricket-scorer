Delete your JCL Cricket Scorer account

This page lets you permanently delete your Cricket Scorer by JCL account and the cloud match records saved under your Google account.

Important — this cannot be undone.
  • Your JCL sign-in account will be deleted from Firebase Authentication.
  • Your saved private match records and their public PRISM overlay records will be deleted.
  • You will lose access to those cloud matches. Export anything you want to keep before continuing.

Checking sign-in status…

For security, Google will ask you to verify your account again before deletion. If deletion fails, the page will show the reason and your data will not be reported as deleted.
